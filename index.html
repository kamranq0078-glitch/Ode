Who He Was

A proud spirit, sweet daily rituals, and unwavering devotion.

A Proud & Steadfast Man

Daddy was a proud man, carrying himself with deep self-respect, quiet stature, and an honorable grace. He never compromised on his dignity. Yet beneath that proud demeanor was a heart that belonged entirely to others — whenever anyone was in need, he was always the first to show up. A dependable rock for everyone around him, always present, without ever asking for praise.

Dignity and honor

Chocolates at the Window

Every single day, without fail, he made sure there was sweetness in the house. He would walk up, gently knock on Ainain’s window, and wait with that warm, knowing smile. When the window opened, out came chocolates — handed out generously not just to her, but to all the kids gathered around. A daily ritual of pure warmth that turned every ordinary afternoon into something magical.

Every single day

From HMT to Sopore

Every week, rain or shine, cold or warm, he made the long journey from HMT all the way to Sopore to attend the Ijtimas. What made him remarkable was his fierce, unwavering independence — he never asked for anyone’s help, never asked for a ride, and never burdened a soul. His devotion was walked with his own two feet and his quiet determination, trusting only in Allah.

Fierce independence & faith

Always There, Forever Her Daddy

He gave everything he had to the people he loved, and never asked for a single thing in return. For Ainain, he was a constant fortress of love. Even now, across the miles between Kashmir and Bangalore, the memory of his proud footsteps, the knock on her window, and the devotion in his voice remains her greatest comfort.

Forever in her heart
KASHMIR WITH LOVE

My dearest Ainain,

I know how heavy your heart feels right now, sitting in Bangalore, so many miles away from Kashmir, from HMT, from the cold mountain air, and from the quiet home where you grew up calling him Daddy.

Grief feels twice as sharp when you are far from home. It arrives in the quiet moments between calls, in the evening when the city outside moves too fast, and in the sudden longing to look over at the window and hear that familiar, gentle knock — remembering him standing there with chocolates in his hands for you and all the kids.

Daddy was such a proud, honorable man. He was always there for everyone, yet so fiercely independent that he would journey from HMT to Sopore every week for his Ijtimas without ever asking for anyone’s help. That same quiet strength, that same dignity and generous heart, lives on right inside you.

I made this little room for you so you never have to feel that distance. Whenever you miss him, you can step into this sanctuary, close your eyes, and listen to the naat he sang in his final days. It is Daddy's voice, soft and unbroken, preserved here just for you.

Please remember, my love: you are never alone in this grief. Daddy’s love did not end when he closed his eyes. It lives on in your gentle nature, in the way you care for everyone around you, in your sisters, and in every prayer your family whispers. He was so proud of you, and he is watching over you with the same warmth he always had.

Always here beside you,
With all my love 🤍
